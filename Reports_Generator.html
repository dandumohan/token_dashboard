Monthly Reports Generator

Fill the officer details, month and values. Cumulative = previous cumulative + during the month is calculated for you. Preview all files (the three letters appear together in one view), then generate the individual files.

1 · Officer & month

Letter date (Rc.No Dt.) is set to the following month and the financial year is derived from the month.

2 · Excel returns (optional: upload this month's .xlsx, otherwise the built-in August 2026 data is used)

Uploading an .xls? Open it in Excel and use Save As → .xlsx first, so cell formatting is kept.

3 · Add your own covering letter or Excel format (optional)

Word (.docx) files are added as covering letters; Excel (.xlsx) files get the month/officer updates plus their own value-entry table. They are remembered in this browser. Standard patterns (From/To block, "for the month of …", Rc.No date, Sd/- signature) update automatically; you can also place tags such as {{name}} {{designation}} {{place}} {{month}} {{year}} {{month_year}} {{fy}} {{next_month}} {{next_year}} {{last_date}} {{sig_name}} {{place_upper}} {{month_upper}} anywhere in the file.
